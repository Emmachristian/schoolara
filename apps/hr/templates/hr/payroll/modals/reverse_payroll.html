What Happens After Reversal
  1. Payroll status will change to REVERSED
  2. Effective net pay and gross pay will become {{ 0|money }}
  3. Original amounts remain visible for audit purposes
  4. All child records (allowances, deductions, bonuses) remain but show zero effective amounts
  5. Reversal details (who, when, why) are permanently recorded
    6. {% if payroll.journal_entry %}
  6. A reversal journal entry will be created
    7. {% endif %}
  7. The payroll cannot be edited, approved, or processed after reversal
  8. This record will be kept for audit trail and cannot be deleted
