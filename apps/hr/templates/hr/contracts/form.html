Quick Tips
Permanent contracts don't require an end date
Fixed-term, probation, and temporary contracts must have an end date
Basic salary is interpreted based on salary frequency
Contract is legally binding only after approval and signing
Upload the signed document PDF for a complete record
Important
Staff members can only have one active contract at a time
Salary changes after signing require a salary history entry
Terminated contracts are kept for audit trail — they cannot be deleted
