Quick Guide
DRAFT vs PENDING: DRAFT invoices can be edited and deleted. PENDING invoices are finalized and create accounting entries.
Scholarships: Auto-applying scholarships will immediately reduce the invoice amount based on active awards.
Validation: Use validation options to prevent duplicate or problematic invoices.
Review First: Always review DRAFT invoices before finalizing to PENDING status.
Important Notes
Once an invoice is finalized to PENDING, it creates accounting entries and cannot be easily deleted.
Invoice numbers are auto-generated when the invoice is created.
