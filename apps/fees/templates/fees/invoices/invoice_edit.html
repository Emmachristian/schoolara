Quick Guide
Unit Amount: Base price per unit
Quantity: Number of units (usually 1 for fees)
Tax %: VAT or other tax percentage
Discount: Regular discount amount (not percentage)
Scholarship: Auto-calculated, cannot be edited directly
Calculation
Amount = Unit Amount × Quantity
Total Discount = Regular + Scholarship
Taxable = Amount - Total Discount
Tax = Taxable × (Tax % / 100)
Final = Taxable + Tax
