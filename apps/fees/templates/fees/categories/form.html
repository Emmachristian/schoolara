Fee Category Configuration Guide
Common Fee Categories

  • Tuition Fee: Core educational charges
  • Library Fee: Access to library resources
  • Laboratory Fee: Science lab equipment and materials
  • Computer Lab Fee: IT lab usage and maintenance
  • Examination Fee: Assessment and grading costs

  • Boarding Fee: Accommodation charges
  • Meals Fee: Food and dining services
  • Laundry Fee: Washing and cleaning services
  • Security Fee: Safety and security services

  • Transport Fee: Bus and transportation
  • Uniform Fee: School uniforms and dress code items
  • Medical Fee: Health services and insurance
  • ID Card Fee: Student identification
  • Sports Fee: Athletic activities and equipment
Configuration Guidelines
Setting Recommended Notes
Frequency Termly Most fees are charged per term
Applicability All Students Unless fee is specific to group
Mandatory Yes Most academic fees are required
Partial Payment Yes Helps parents manage payments
Refundable Depends Academic fees usually not refundable
Taxable Check Laws Varies by country/region
Best Practices
  • Use consistent naming conventions
  • Create specific categories rather than generic ones
  • Set appropriate applicability rules
  • Consider parent/student understanding
  • Group related categories in display groups
  • Set reasonable display orders
  • Test with sample invoices before going live
