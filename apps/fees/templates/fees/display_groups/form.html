Organization Tips
  • Use clear, descriptive names
  • Group related fees logically
  • Order groups by importance
  • Keep groups focused and specific
  • Consider parent/student perspective
  • Limit to 6-8 groups maximum
Color Recommendations
  • Use distinct colors for easy identification
  • Consider color psychology (green for money, blue for trust)
  • Ensure good contrast for readability
  • Keep colors consistent across sessions
  • Test colors in both print and digital formats
Display Order Guidelines
  • 1-10: Core academic fees
  • 11-20: Boarding and accommodation
  • 21-30: Transportation
  • 31-40: Activities and events
  • 41-50: Supplies and materials
  • 51+: Miscellaneous fees
